# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Liberty Browser community to be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and different viewpoints with patience.
- Protect privacy: do not post anyone's credentials, cookies, browser profile, or private account data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private data, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include private browser data in a report. Maintainers will review reports and take action appropriate to the circumstances.
